Absolute-yield uncertainty

Absolute DF efficiency is the pilot-derived constant: the El-Kebeer et al. pilot yield, read as 6.48 m³ H₂ per m³ of sludge at 39.7 kg COD m⁻³ (163 mL H₂ g⁻¹ COD, 0.349 of the Thauer yield). The source reports its yield in internally inconsistent units, and this reading is the dominant uncertainty. Under the alternative reading (~23 mL H₂ g⁻¹ COD), DF output would be about 7-fold lower (total output about 3.4-fold lower with the PF stage unchanged) and LCOH correspondingly higher. Part 1 reports both readings. See Methods.

Surrogate and equations

Ridge regression (α = 10; Medium training envelope) on logit(η) with z-scored inputs: intercept −1.2223; βOLR +0.5548 (mean 2.9304, s.d. 1.1671); βNH₄ +0.0049 (51.426, 15.954); βALK +0.0026 (1,829.9, 358.74). η̂(x) = min(0.55, 1 / (1 + e−(intercept + Σβz))). Held-out RMSE 0.067 in ηDF; CV-R² 0.71.

Anchored ηDF = min(0.55, 0.349 × η̂(x) / η̂(xGEA)), xGEA = OLR 1.0, NH₄⁺ 50, alkalinity 1,800. H₂ = [13,514 × (ηDF/0.349) + 2,881] × (V/82,500 m³) m³ d⁻¹ (Gabal El-Asfar DF and PF stages, case-study Monte Carlo means).

Surrogate domain (training envelope): OLR 1.0–5.0 kg COD m⁻³ d⁻¹; NH₄⁺ 25–80 mg L⁻¹; alkalinity 1,200–2,500 mg CaCO₃ L⁻¹; 20–38 °C; municipal primary sludge; influent ≥100,000 m³ d⁻¹.
